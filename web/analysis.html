C. Safety impact 2: moving or stopped?

Safety impact 2 means the problem could cause loss of control or a crash. By our rule, the same problem gets the same score whether the car was moving or stopped. This chart shows when these complaints actually happened. To decide what to look at first, start with the moving ones (e.g. highway driving).

D. Do hybrid and gas cars differ?

Bars show percentages, not counts. This data does not tell how many gas and hybrid cars are on the road, so counts cannot be compared directly. Each bar is the share within that car's own complaints: These are shares among complaints people chose to file. They do not show that one car type is more dangerous.


Plug-in hybrid and unknown powertrain are left out because there are too few. Click a bar to see those complaints on the search page.
